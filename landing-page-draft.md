## Copy

### Hero
**Headline:** Never lose track of a prayer again.
**Subhead:** A simple, private way to log your five daily prayers, catch up on missed ones (qada), and — if you're a parent — help your whole family build the habit together.
**CTA button:** Join the waitlist

### Value prop bullets
- **Log in seconds.** Tap a prayer when it's done. That's the whole interaction.
- **Catch up without guilt.** A qada tracker calculates what you owe and helps you chip away at it, prayer by prayer.
- **Built for families.** One parent dashboard, every child's progress, no shared login mess.

### How it works (3 steps)
1. **Log** — Mark each of your five daily prayers as you pray them.
2. **Track** — See your qada debt shrink and get an honest completion estimate.
3. **Share** — Parents get a live view of every child's progress, without hovering.

### Family mode teaser
**Heading:** One dashboard for the whole household.
**Body:** Add each child's profile, see their daily progress at a glance, and let them log on their own device with a simple PIN — their data stays theirs, never mixed up with a sibling's.

### FAQ (draft — refine once product is closer to done)
- **Is this free?** A free tier covers one profile. A paid tier unlocks family/multi-child support and extended stats.
- **Does it work without internet?** Logging works offline; prayer times need an initial location fetch.
- **How are prayer times calculated?** By your location and your chosen madhhab, via a standard calculation method.
- **Is my data private?** Yes — your prayer log is yours. See our privacy policy for details.
- **What is qada?** Missed prayers that are made up later. The app estimates your qada debt and helps you track paying it down.
- **Which platforms are supported?** iOS and Android at launch.

### Email capture
**Heading:** Be first to know when we launch.
**Placeholder:** you@email.com
**Button:** Notify me
**Microcopy below:** No spam — just a launch email and the occasional update.
